About Me

Twenty years between southern ice and Mont Blanc granite.

I'm Diego Simari, a certified IFMGA/UIAGM Mountain Guide. I split the year between Chamonix, where I guide in the Mont Blanc massif, and Patagonia, where I guide ski adventures and climbing trips.

I work with climbers taking their first steps on a rope team as well as with teams planning multi-week expeditions in remote ranges. Every program is built to measure: technical level, time available, and what each person is looking for in the mountains.
