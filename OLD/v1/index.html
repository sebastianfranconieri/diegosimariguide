About Me

Twenty years between southern ice and Mont Blanc granite.

I'm Diego Simari, a certified IFMGA/UIAGM Mountain Guide. I split the year between Chamonix, where I guide classic rock and ice routes in the Mont Blanc massif, and Patagonia, where I spend the southern summers between El Chaltén and Torres del Paine. My job is to read the mountain — the weather, the snow, the state of the group — and make, trip after trip, the simplest decision of all: get up there and come back well.

I work with climbers taking their first steps on a rope team as well as with teams planning multi-week expeditions in remote ranges. Every program is built to measure: technical level, time available, and what each person is looking for in the mountains.

  • IFMGA - UIAGM — Certified Mountain Guide
  • 20+ years of experience in the Alps and Patagonia
  • Based in Chamonix Mont Blanc and Patagonia Argentina
